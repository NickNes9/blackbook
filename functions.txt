BLACK BOOK - USER CAPABILITIES
==============================

Personal finance tracking app. Everything below is what the user can do
in the front-end.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
GLOBAL / ALL PAGES
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- Navigate between pages: Overview, Bills, Budget, Credit Cards,
  Savings, Debts, Invoices, Forecast, Settings (sidebar, or press 1-9).
- Switch pages with the mouse wheel over the sidebar.
- Jump straight to today with a keypress (D).
- Move between months with the left/right arrow keys; scroll the wheel
  over the month picker to change the displayed month/year.
- Hide/enable any page from Settings ("Show page" toggles); pages are
  removed from the sidebar and warnings disappear when disabled.
- Run a command palette (Ctrl+K or Space) to do things by typing:
    * "50 coffee"           - log a 50 unit expense on a category
    * "-120 rent"           - log income (negative = income entry)
    * "pay <bill name>"     - pay a bill
    * "bg <category> <amt>" - set a budget amount (e.g. bg shop 20000)
    * "bg <category> clear" - remove a budget
    * "tr <amt> <from> <to>"- transfer money between accounts
    * "dep <amt> <goal>"    - deposit into a savings goal
    * "wd <amt> <goal>"     - withdraw from a savings goal
    * "csv"                 - export data as CSV
    * "json"                - export data as JSON
    * "profiles"            - manage profiles
- Keyboard shortcuts (when not typing in a field):
    * A / T  - open transaction / transfer modals
    * D      - go to today
    * C      - copy one selected or hovered transaction into a new draft dated today
      Saving a copy selects its new entry and shows a temporary confirmation.
    * E      - bulk edit selected transactions
    * M      - merge selected transactions
    * B      - toggle bulk "selected only" filter
    * Ctrl+Z - undo the latest supported financial change in this session
    * Ctrl+Shift+Z - redo an undone change in this session
  Undo is temporary, keeps at most 100 recent changes, and is cleared when
  the app session ends. The footer only shows shortcuts available on the
  current page and for the current selection.
    * H      - show/hide the graph on the current page
    * Tab    - cycle through the displayed accounts
    * Space  - open command palette
    * Delete - delete the selected transactions
    * Y/N    - confirm/dismiss dialogs
- Live save indicator in the header (SAVED / SAVING / UNSAVED).
- Transaction selection uses whole-row highlighting and a left-edge marker in
  Overview, Find & Replace, Recurring Payments, and the merge target picker.
  The tint and marker follow Settings > Appearance > Highlight Color.
- Check for an app update on each launch. A new release appears as an
  UPDATE AVAILABLE version card in the top-center header; opening it
  navigates to About & Updates, and its X dismisses it for this run.
- Attention bar in the header flags urgent items (overdue bills and forecast
  shortfalls). Click an alert to jump to it;
  more than 3 alerts is summarized and expandable.
- Data is stored per-profile on the server; all changes auto-save.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERVIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- See all accounts at a glance (colored chip cards with balance, shown
  in base currency).
- Click an account chip to open a panel showing that account's balance
  and per-category detail; dismiss the panel with an X.
- Hide accounts from the overview via the panel (hidden accounts keep
  their transactions but stop showing).
- Open YEARLY BALANCES from the account-management panel to review each
  year’s START, END, and CHANGE. A selected account shows a native-currency
  start/end graph above a newest-first, scrollable history. “All accounts”
  offers TOTAL and ACCOUNTS views: TOTAL combines start/end by year in the
  profile base currency; ACCOUNTS graphs each selected account’s END in that
  same currency. Conversion uses current rates and is labeled accordingly;
  accounts with missing rates are omitted from combined values with a warning.
  The current-year summary is through today. Hidden and closed accounts remain
  selectable. In ACCOUNTS view, click a card to toggle it, Shift-click to
  toggle while preserving other selections, or Alt-click to isolate it.
- A recorded opening checkpoint is applied before ordinary January 1 activity;
  the start includes only its designated opening correction. Other January 1
  transactions remain ordinary activity. Checkpoint status is shown once in
  the account context, without exposing stored target values.
- Pick a displayed month/year with the month picker.
- Monthly summary: income, expenses, and net for the displayed month.
- Category breakdown bars showing where this month's money went.
- Filter the transaction list by:
    * Category filter chips (All / each category); click for one category,
      Shift-click to add/remove several, and Alt-click to isolate while
      clearing the type filter. Removing the final selection restores All.
    * A type toggle cycling TYPE -> EXPENSES -> INCOME.
    * "Selected (n)" chip to show only bulk-selected rows.
- Transaction list rows with account stripe, date, category, note,
  action buttons (EDIT / DELETE) and the amount.
- Sort the list by date, value, or description/name, ascending/descending.
- Transfers appear as special rows with both amounts (out -> in).
- Click a row to bulk-select; shift-click selects a range; click outside
  the list to clear; bulk-edit, merge, or delete the selection.
- E and Delete act on selected transactions first. With nothing selected,
  they act on the row under the pointer.
- After merging, a temporary top-bar notice confirms how many rows merged.
- Edit transactions, delete them, or open a transaction's linked bill /
  debt / invoice / savings / card plan from its row.
- Graph "INCOME VS EXPENSES" (12-month lines), draggable to resize;
  H key or the footer button shows/hides it.
- This month's income/expense donut shows one percentage in its center.
  Click the percentage to switch between income (green) and expenses
  (red); hover a slice for its exact amount and share in a floating
  tooltip above the chart.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
TRANSACTIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- Add a new transaction (expense or income) with amount, category,
  account, currency, date, and note. Amount parsing understands chained
  math expressions with normal precedence (e.g. "100-5*2-10") and
  multiple comma decimals (e.g. "10,5-2,5+1").
- The + / − amount button chooses income or expense. New transactions
  remember the last button choice per profile; a typed leading sign
  overrides it for that entry. Editing starts from the saved type. The
  colored button is square and separated from the amount field.
- Transactions can store an exact account-side amount/currency plus an optional
  exact secondary amount/currency. Editing preserves both entered values rather
  than reconstructing either side from a changing exchange rate.
- New transactions show Account, Date, and signed Amount on the first row.
  Purchase Currency defaults to the account currency; choosing a different
  currency reveals the exact purchase amount field below.
- Transaction entry places Account, Category, and Date on the first row, then
  primary and purchase amounts on identical aligned rows with shared sign
  buttons. The purchase amount stays visible but disabled until its currency
  differs from the primary. The primary currency is shown as text; the purchase
  currency remains selectable. The category
  picker has a visible CATEGORY label and keeps its previous width.
- Transfer money between any two accounts (with optional currency
  conversion and note). Note updates as accounts change.
- Edit any transaction or transfer later.
- Delete transactions; linked bill/debt/invoice/savings records are kept
  consistent (bill stays unpaid, amount removed, etc.).
- Bulk select transactions to: edit many at once (type, category, date,
  currency, note...), merge into a single transaction, merge as a
  transfer, or bulk-delete.
- Speed-dial style date input understands the configured date separator;
  smart dates (numeric offsets) are accepted.
- Date display format is configurable in Settings → Appearance using
  D/DD, M/MM/MMM, and YY/YYYY tokens (MMM is the longest month name).

----------------------------------------------------------------------
BILLS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- Add / edit / delete recurring bills: name, amount (optional), currency,
  due day, color, category, pay-from account, and whether they appear in
  the graph.
- Enable AUTO-PAY so the bill pays itself on its due day.
- Pay a bill for a month from the bills graph/grid - a transaction is
  created automatically and linked to that bill+month.
- Default vs custom amounts (pay a different sum for a specific month).
- Pick which account a payment is drawn from (and it converts currency
  if needed).
- Undo/toggle a payment - removes the linked transaction.
- Toggle bills on/off in the graph (chips); Shift-click preserves multiple
  selections, and Alt-click isolates one bill. Toggle the TOTAL line.
- The graph's TOTAL line starts hidden. Bill month cells show full amounts
  whenever the rendered number fits with an 8 px inner margin; only values
  that cannot fit use compact amounts and currency symbols where available.
  Full values remain available on hover.
- 12-month graph "BILLS BY MONTH" per bill, plus a monthly bill total.
- Selected-year bill breakdown donut beside the monthly lines. It follows
  the same bill filters and shows amounts and percentages on hover.
- Repair / re-link bill payments to their transactions automatically.
- Reorder bills with up/down controls; bill order is stored in the profile.
- Paid bill cells show an up/down arrow versus the previous month (previous
  year for annual bills). Hover shows the exact difference and percentage.
  Comparisons require recorded amounts in the same bill currency.
- Monthly totals are summarized per month.
- Graph resize + show/hide like other pages.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
BUDGET
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- Set a monthly budget limit per category (SET/EDIT button on each card).
  Editing a budget takes effect immediately on that card.
- The budget donut sits beside the 12-month spending graph. It shows the
  selected month's category shares and any unassigned portion of the total;
  hovering a slice shows its exact amount and percentage.
- Use ALLOCATE to open a modal with category cards and a donut preview.
  Add/remove categories and edit either amount or percentage; the donut
  updates live. The allocation total sits below the donut. RESET PLAN
  clears the shares to 100% unassigned without changing the total;
  SAVE PLAN can keep that unassigned state.
  The plan starts in the selected month and carries forward. While active,
  it replaces individual category limits in the monthly total, but keeps
  them saved. Use Individual Limits stops the plan from the selected month
  without erasing older plans.
- See spent (or earned, for income categories), limit, percentage used, and
  a progress bar per category.
- Expense categories over budget turn red (number + bar) when spend > 100%.
- Income categories show the percentage in green once they have earned at
  least their budget; a net-positive income category still below budget is
  red, and an income category whose net total is not positive shows the
  percentage in the default text color.
- Total budgeted vs total spent is summarized for the month.
- Cards without a budget are still listed so you can add one on the spot.
- Choose which categories appear in the graph (chips below the graph).
  Click toggles a category, Shift-click toggles while preserving multiple
  selections, and Alt-click isolates one category.
  Only categories with spending during the displayed year appear. Toggle
  the TOTAL line on/off (hidden by default).
- 12-month "SPEND BY CATEGORY" line graph, draggable to resize. Hovering a
  month shows exact values without needing to hit a tiny point.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
CREDIT CARDS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- Add / edit / delete credit cards (with limit, color, currency).
- Log a card purchase directly (category, note, date) - you get asked
  whether to spread it into installments.
- Create an installment plan: months, interest %, and funding account.
  Installment purchases are paid automatically each month into a linked
  transaction.
- Pay installments in one or more amounts; partial amounts accumulate on
  that installment and show paid amount / amount due. A slot becomes paid
  when the full amount is covered; custom plan payments fill slots in order.
- Mark "Advance paid" - funding the card with a lump sum makes installments
  convert into a single up-front payment per month.
- Track installments month by month (seq / total), fully-paid plans are
  shown closed with a checkmark.
- Open payment history to see installment slots and their linked transactions;
  visually track progress with amount-weighted markers on the paid % bar.
- Chart shows installment plan amounts over months.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SAVINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- Create savings goals with a target amount and currency.
- Set an optional target date; the goal shows the amount needed per calendar
  month, including the current and target months, or a target-passed/reached state.
- Add deposits and withdrawals per goal (with date and note); each entry
  creates an automatic transaction in the ledger.
- Goal totals and progress stay visible; expand Payment History to see
  entries and their transaction links. Delete entries from their history.
- Total savings progress bar across all goals.
- Pick the display year on this page (its own year picker).
- Edit/delete goals entirely.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
DEBTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- Track debts: person, type (owed-to-me vs I-owe), amount, currency,
  date, due date, note, category.
- Mark a debt as settled when paid off.
- Record payments (default amount or custom) - each creates a linked
  transaction.
- Debt totals, progress, dates, and notes stay visible; expand Payment
  History to see linked transactions. Delete individual linked payment
  transactions from the history.
- Filter debts by direction (chips + cycle button).
- Summary of totals for each direction and what's overdue.
- Colors/bars show remaining proportion.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
INVOICES
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- Create invoices with a party, number (NNN / YEAR auto-increments),
  currency, issue date, optional paid date, direction (incoming/outgoing),
  note and category.
- Save and reuse per-profile invoice templates containing party, direction,
  currency, category, note and line items. Invoice number, dates, payment
  status and linked file are not copied into a new invoice.
- Add/remove line items (description, qty, price); total is computed
  automatically and validation blocks empty invoices.
- Use + LINK FILE to choose the original file in the computer's file picker.
  The invoice stores its file path and name only; the INVOICE button opens
  that original in the computer's default app. The file is never copied into
  Black Book. Moving it requires linking it again. Legacy browser-stored
  attachments remain readable where the browser still has access.
- Edit invoices; changing totals/currency/direction re-validates existing
  payments first.
- Record payments (default or custom amount) with a chosen paid date; the
  linked transaction uses that same date. Clicking PAID opens this form,
  prefilled with the remaining balance and the invoice date or saved paid
  date. Track PAID / PARTIAL / UNPAID state.
- Mark a paid invoice unpaid (removes its linked payment transactions).
- Expand a card to see line-item detail; delete invoices (removes linked
  payment transactions).
- Filter by direction; next sequence number is suggested for new
  invoices.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
FORECAST
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- See the forecasted cash balance over the coming months (lines per
  account, plus total).
- Toggle forecast accounts with their colored chips; Shift-click preserves
  multiple selections, and Alt-click isolates one account.
- Graph values appear in a floating tooltip above the chart, consistent
  with the Overview and Budget donuts; the tooltip follows the hovered
  point or slice and stays within the viewport.
- Forecast accounts for recurring templates (Salary, Rent, etc.),
  recurring bills, savings goals, and card plans. Unpaid invoices and open
  personal debts stay in their own pages and are not included in the projection.
- Open a recurring template from the forecast to edit it.
- Forecast warnings for projected shortfalls; show them in a dialog.
- Auto-balanced bill payments are folded into the projection.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SETTINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- Sections appear in this order, including on narrow screens: Profiles,
  Appearance, Data (including Import Rules), Defaults, Pages, Accounts,
  Categories, Exchange Rates, About & Updates.

Profiles:
- Create a new profile (name + optional password), switch between
  profiles, edit the active profile name and base currency together,
  delete profiles, and set/remove a password. Open an inactive profile
  before editing it.
- Passwords lock a profile; locked profiles need the password at load.
  Two seconds of disconnection don't lock you out; a dropdown picker
  selects the profile to unlock.
- Default profile (none required) shows no lock button.

Accounts:
- Add / edit / delete accounts: name, description, short name, color,
  currency, foreign fee %, type (cash / bank / credit card).
- Credit cards get installment interest % + due day fields.
- Installment interest and due day appear only for Credit Card; foreign
  fee appears only when the account currency differs from the profile
  base currency. Hidden saved fee values remain intact.
- Reorder accounts; delete is disabled for protected accounts.

Categories:
- Add / edit / delete transaction categories (name + color, or AUTO).
- Protected categories can't be deleted.

Currencies & rates:
- Base currency per profile (chosen at creation or in the active
  profile's Edit modal); changing it requires rates for both currencies.
- Enable extra currencies; set or refresh their exchange rates
  (live fetch or manual "set rate" for a chosen date).
- Rates table with per-day historical rate entries; manual rates take
  precedence over fetched ones.
- Each currency occupies one row; narrow screens use compact action icons
  so the rates list does not wrap or scroll sideways. The default currency
  reserves a blank removal slot to keep dates and actions aligned.
- Exchange Rates keeps the manual RATE action but no longer changes the
  profile's base currency from its rows.

CSV:
- Import transactions from CSV with column mapping (auto-guessed):
  date, type, amount (+ sign detection), currency, account, card,
  category, note. Date formats are normalized; you can also hide the
  import summary preview if wanted.
- Import rules: auto-assign a category when the description matches
  ("IF DESCRIPTION HAS 'x' -> category").
- Export the current profile to CSV or JSON (also from the command
  palette).
- Import a JSON profile after reviewing its account/category/transaction
  counts and confirming replacement; the current profile is preserved in
  its BACKUPS folder before the import is written.

Appearance / behavior:
- Dark/light theme with accent + income/expense color pickers.
- Date format uses D/DD, M/MM/MMM, and YY/YYYY tokens; the default is
  DD/MM/YYYY and month names can be abbreviated with MMM.
- Undo/redo recent financial changes with Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z; history is
  session-only and capped at 100 changes.
- The footer shows only keyboard shortcuts available on the current page
  and for the current selection.
- Space-bar/Enter-driven dialogs and pre-filled forms.
- Attention bar can be shown/hidden.
- Its Settings toggle shows the current state: highlight-colored SHOW when
  enabled and gray HIDE when disabled.

Data health:
- Data tools (Check Data, Find & Replace, People) occupy the first action row;
  export and import controls appear below a divider with spacing.
- Find & Replace is available on Overview and Settings > Data. Filter by
  description, account, category, type, date range, or person. Preview all
  changes and exclude rows before applying; Ctrl+Z reverses the batch.
- Replace matching text literally, or rewrite the full description using
  #description, #date, #month, #year, #account, and #category.
  Replace Text has separate Text to replace and New text fields, each spanning
  two columns. Rewrite Description replaces the entire description with the
  New description.
  Preview and Apply are right-aligned, with the selection count on the left.
  Filter fields, dropdowns, and square toggles share a uniform height and
  align to the same grid rows.
  Apply keeps the modal open and refreshes results without resetting filters.
- Custom closes Find & Replace and applies its search filters to Overview,
  including whole-word and exclusion toggles. It filters transactions, summary,
  category breakdown, and graphs while months/accounts remain navigable. A
  highlighted Find & Replace button indicates the active session filter;
  clicking it clears that filter without adding extra controls to Overview.
- Description contains has a Partial / Whole word toggle. Partial matches
  inside words; Whole word matches complete words or phrases, case-insensitively.
  A square boundary-marked ab icon beside the field toggles this: highlighted
  means Whole word, gray means Partial, with a tooltip explaining the state.
  The toggle affects the search filter, not Text to replace.
- Person, Category, and Account filters include No person / No category /
  No account options for transactions without those assignments.
- Square not-equal toggles beside Person, Category, and Account invert their
  selected filter. Highlighted means exclude the selected value; gray means
  include it. With All selected the toggle has no filtering effect. Inverting
  No person/category/account finds transactions with that assignment present.
- Search and change previews update live after a short typing delay. With all
  filters at defaults, the results stay empty. Any active filter (including an
  account without description text) shows matching transactions. Replacement
  fields are not required to search. Rows start unselected; only selected rows
  are highlighted. With no selection, Apply affects all matching changed rows;
  otherwise it affects only the selected changed rows. The count shows the scope.
  Results show transactions with pending changes first, newest-first within
  the changed and unchanged groups.
  Description contains remains a separate filter. People use @Name mentions.
- Assign Person previews matching transactions and adds the chosen person
  without removing existing people. Optional Name to replace changes that name
  to an @Name mention, preserving dates and all other description text. Matching
  is case-insensitive and does not replace names inside longer words. Unchanged,
  already-associated rows are skipped. Apply saves one undoable batch.
- Find & Replace also supports Set Category, Delete Transactions, Remove
  Person (one or all), Link to Bill, and Set Account (including an explicitly
  selected transfer side). Every action previews changes and skipped rows.
  Delete requires confirmation and updates linked financial records. Bill
  linking marks recorded months paid without creating transactions. Account
  changes preserve exact stored currency amounts, swapping primary/secondary
  values when appropriate; incompatible currencies and protected payment
  records require individual editing. Each batch is undoable.
- Settings > Data > People manages each profile's directory. Names, colors,
  optional account numbers, and notes stay in the profile. Person mentions are
  bold and use their saved color in transaction lists, search, and data-review
  views. Type @ in a transaction or
  transfer description and select a suggestion to associate a person.
- Person history links to transactions; income, expenses, and transfers are
  summarized separately by currency. Shared entries show their full amounts.
- People and Find & Replace use fixed-height modals with internal scrolling;
  the page behind a modal cannot scroll. The selected person uses the highlight color.
- People places Name and Color together, followed by account number and notes,
  with right-aligned save/delete actions. A divider separates the name list;
  selected names use inverse text color. Income/expense/net are color-coded,
  followed by a transaction count and scrollable history with icon-only links.
- Settings > Data > CHECK DATA opens Data Tools with CHECK DATA and
  RECURRING PAYMENTS tabs.
- Data Tools uses a wide fixed-height modal, compact summaries and zebra rows.
  Issues expand to show details/repairs; a clean scan shows No Issues Found.
  Recurring groups show Match Text, Bulk Edit and Disregard Match together,
  followed by bill linking and category-rule controls together on one row,
  wrapping when the window is narrow;
  saved rules are collapsed at the bottom. Lists scroll below the fixed tabs.
  Bulk Edit opens Find & Replace instead of separate group editing controls and
  carries the group's transactions (or selected subset) into the live editing
  workflow. Its visible group scope can be cleared, refined with filters, or
  carried to Overview through Custom.
  The modal matches Find & Replace at 1050px. Group totals are right-aligned
  in their own column. Entries show date, category, description, amount and
  Edit, with amounts right-aligned and spaced apart from the button.
- Check Data reviews broken links, duplicate IDs, possible duplicate
  transactions, and missing account rates. Broken transaction account links
  can be repaired by choosing a replacement account; repairs are undoable and
  the report refreshes after saving. Existing red link icons still open the
  report for the affected record.
- A Minimum Entries slider hides smaller recurring groups, defaulting to 10.
  Its fixed range is 1-12. The label updates immediately; groups refresh after
  a short typing/dragging delay to avoid jumping. It shows the visible group count. The threshold
  remains while using the app session; hidden groups are not deleted.
- Recurring Payments suggests likely repeated transactions by description,
  using meaningful shared words rather than loose substring/first-word groups.
  Routine dates, months, and reference numbers are ignored for grouping; original
  descriptions stay untouched. Matching descriptions form one group across
  accounts, currencies, and income/expenses, even with irregular timing. Rows
  identify their account and type; currency ranges stay separate. Timing is a
  suggestion within each account/type/currency subgroup, not an inclusion gate.
  Bill actions use expense entries only; linked payments remain excluded.
  payment source, currency, and cadence. Amounts may vary. Suggestions never
  change data automatically; selected entries can be reviewed, bulk edited,
  merged with confirmation, or used to prefill a bill. Expense groups of any
  detected cadence can become bills; non-annual cadences map to monthly bills.
- Repeated charges stay separate unless the user explicitly chooses to merge
  selected entries. Ordinary recurring card charges can become bills paid from
  that card. Historical charges are not converted to installment plans.
- New installations start empty and prompt you to create a profile.

Updates:
- Updates match checksums by platform filename and validate archive paths
  and core files. Unrelated files are preserved; failed writes roll back.
  Unchanged files skip installation. Downloads still use full ZIPs.
- Check for / install app updates (header notice, manual check button, and a
  restart flow). During an update the UI shows install status.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
DATA MODEL HIGHLIGHTS (for reference)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- profiles/<name>.json holds everything for a profile.
- Data keys: accounts, categories, creditCards, transactions, balanceCheckpoints,
  billPayments, budgets, budgetAllocations, savingsGoals, debts, invoices, installments,
  invoiceTemplates, recurringTemplates, importRules, recurringRules,
  reconciliations (legacy data preserved), settings. Recurring rules can
  categorize matching transactions or suppress matching recurrence suggestions;
  rules are saved within that profile. Recurring review also links detected
  historical payments to existing or newly created bills without creating
  duplicate transactions. A bill month can aggregate several linked entries,
  including split payments across accounts/cards and currencies; its displayed
  total is converted to the profile base currency, while the link opens all
  contributing transactions selected on Overview. The primary transaction
  reference remains the largest linked payment. Similar
  recurring descriptions are grouped and
  sorted by name; unifying descriptions updates notes only and never combines
  or removes transaction amounts.
- Optional balanceCheckpoints record user-provided opening or current balance
  anchors. Opening corrections are dated January 1 but represent the balance
  before ordinary activity on that date. Settlement-aware account history
  keeps one full transfer transaction while applying its validated balance
  parts on their actual dates; it does not rewrite the transaction’s date or
  split it into duplicate transfer records.
- Transactions can be plain, transfers (from/to accounts), bill payments,
  invoice payments, debt payments, savings entries, or installment
  payments - all cross-linked so deletions stay consistent.
- Transaction entries can preserve an exact original amount and currency
  alongside the account amount. The original amount stays disabled until its
  currency differs from the account currency.
